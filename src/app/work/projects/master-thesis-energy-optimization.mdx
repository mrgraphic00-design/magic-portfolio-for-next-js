## Master's Thesis Project

Techno-economic optimization of the OTH Amberg-Weiden campus energy system, focusing on the integration and optimization of renewable generation, energy storage, electric mobility, and sector-coupled energy technologies.

## Project Overview

**Institution:** Ostbayerische Technische Hochschule Amberg-Weiden  
**Duration:** April 2026 - October 2026  
**Status:** Completed

## Objectives

- Optimize the operation and sizing of the campus energy system
- Evaluate the integration of renewable energy technologies
- Assess battery energy storage and electric mobility integration
- Analyze sector coupling between electrical and thermal energy systems
- Minimize system costs and Levelized Cost of Energy (LCOE)
- Evaluate grid peak reduction and renewable energy utilization
- Quantify CO₂ emissions and key system performance indicators


### Energy System Modeling

Developed an hourly energy system optimization model using PyPSA to represent the interaction between electrical and thermal energy systems, including:

- Photovoltaic generation
- Battery energy storage systems (BESS)
- Battery electric vehicle (BEV) charging
- Heat pump
- Combined heat and power (CHP)
- Gas boilers
- Thermal energy storage
- Public grid connection
- Electrical and thermal demand

The model was used to evaluate different system configurations and operating strategies under technical and economic constraints.

### Data Analysis & Optimization

- Python — Data analysis and energy system modelling
- PyPSA — Energy system optimization and simulation
- Pandas & NumPy — Time-series processing and analysis
- Gurobi — Mathematical optimization
- Polysun — PV system simulation
